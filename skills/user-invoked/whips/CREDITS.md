# Credits

Routed-playbook design adapted from [Lauren Tan (poteto)](https://github.com/poteto)'s `pstack` ([cursor/plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins), folder `pstack`): the `poteto-mode` router that matches a task to a playbook, opens its steps verbatim as a todo, routes to leaf skills as steps fire, and delegates implementation to fresh subagents with consolidated scope under a verifying director. Principles 2, 6, 7, and 9 resynced with upstream pstack #633 (principles as reasoning, not rules) and #604 (cheap always-on signal). The `opening-a-pr` playbook adapts pstack's `opening-a-pr` (worktree per unit, small ordered commits, Conventional Commits titles, base-branch stacks, no babysit on open) with readiness following upstream: every PR opens ready, and the verdict (not draft status) gates the merge. The `autopilot-full` and `autopilot-stack` playbooks adapt pstack's autopilot pair (one owner per PR, parallel off trunk, pushed trail, stuck replacement, merge-prep rebase rule, ketchup close) to workers in their own worktrees (OMP being one optional harness among others) with the mode verifier in place of the swarm. The `authoring-a-skill` playbook adapts pstack's `authoring-a-skill` (authoring skill, validation, then opening a PR). The `ship-triage` review mode adapts pstack's `bugbot-triage` fix, dismiss, ask rubric. Playbook shapes (feature checkpoint, bug repro-first, swarm and arena fan-out, interrogate panels, show-me-your-work trail, benny triage plus reproduce-and-fix automations) paraphrased into this repo's HIT flow and skill names. The `multi-phase-plan` playbook plus `check-plan.mjs` adapt pstack's `multi-phase-plan` (plan skeleton plus plan linter) with local worktree owners, CI plus self-proof verdicts, agent landing per risk tier, an optional `needs-human` review gate, and scheduled wakes. Director plus worker mechanics, advisor severities, and hooks gates per [oh-my-pi](https://github.com/can1357/oh-my-pi) docs (`vibe-mode.md`, `advisor-watchdog.md`, `hooks.md`) when OMP is used as the harness. Copied in, not depended on, so standalone installs keep working without the upstream repos.
