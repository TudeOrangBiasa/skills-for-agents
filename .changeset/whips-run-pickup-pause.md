`/whips` gains three playbooks for work nobody is watching. `autonomous-run` states a checkable exit predicate and a budget, wakes through whatever the harness offers, routes each unit through its own playbook to a separate draft PR, and keeps a run log under `.scratch/whips/<run>/`. `pause` stops at a safe boundary, pushes a `wip:` commit, and writes a resume note. `pickup` rebuilds state from that trail and continues at the resume point without redoing finished work. The pause list still applies, so merges stay human.
